🔥 OPTION 1: THE STORYTELLER / IN-DEPTH TECH SHOWCASE (RECOMMENDED)

🚗 Excited to unveil my latest project: The Hanzala Automotive Detailing & Operations Platform! 

Most service-based businesses lose customers at checkout due to complicated booking forms, lack of real-time slot transparency, and clunky check-in processes. I set out to solve this end-to-end by building an enterprise-grade car wash booking and staff management platform designed specifically for luxury automotive studios.

Here is an inside look at what I built and the engineering behind it:

✨ Key Highlights & Features:

🏎️ Luxury Automotive Design System:
Crafted with an Obsidian Dark backdrop, metallic amber/gold accents, and fluid glassmorphism to mirror the high-end feel of premium automotive brands (Porsche, Mercedes-AMG, Audi).

⏱️ 60-Second Multi-Step Booking Engine:
A smooth 5-step wizard allowing customers to choose service packages, vehicle sizes (with dynamic category multipliers), real-time time slots, add-ons, and payment methods seamlessly.

🎫 Instant Digital QR Pass Generation:
Upon checkout confirmation, the system dynamically renders a unique digital QR pass directly on the canvas for rapid on-site scanning and instant customer arrival check-in.

🛡️ Automated Policy & Refund Calculation:
Implemented transparent cancellation management featuring a 5% payment gateway processing deduction formula, complete with an immutable audit log and live refund preview.

🎁 VIP Loyalty & Rewards Engine:
Spend-based rewards points calculator (1 point per Rs. 100 spent) allowing returning customers to redeem instant discounts directly at checkout.

📊 Multi-Role Staff & Admin Operations Portal:
A full operational back-office featuring:
• Real-time bay occupancy and technician assignment
• Live appointment queue with status updates (Scheduled, In Bay, Completed)
• Revenue metrics, analytics charts, and exportable CSV audit logs
• Direct QR ticket scanner simulation for bay attendants

🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture:
• Framework: Vue 3 (Composition API, <script setup>)
• Build Tool: Vite
• Styling: Custom Vanilla CSS Luxury Design System & Glassmorphism
• Dynamic Utilities: Canvas Confetti, QR Code engine, Lucide Icons
• State Management: Centralized Reactive Store with strict client/server price calculation
